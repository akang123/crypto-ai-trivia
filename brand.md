# Merkle Research / Field Note 001

Status: active

## Direction

Strict black, white, and grayscale only. The game should feel like a compact research instrument: editorial, technical, calm, and slightly severe. No neon crypto gradients, no decorative 3D, and no accent colors.

## Palette

- Ink: `#080808`
- Panel: `#151515`
- Raised panel: `#1b1b1b`
- White: `#f5f5f2`
- Soft white: `#d4d4cf`
- Muted: `#a1a19b`
- Line: `#373737`

## Voice

Specific, curious, research-led. Prefer thesis-driven prompts over hype. The recurring editorial frame is: The Signal, The System, The Money, The Players, and The Tension.
